💬 句式句型

Sentence Patterns
越 …… 越 ……
the more … the more …
  • 妹妹越長越漂亮。My little sister grows prettier and prettier.
  • 天氣越來越冷了。The weather is getting colder and colder.
會 …… 還會 ……
can … and can also …
  • 我會講故事，還會唱歌。I can tell stories, and I can also sing.
  • 弟弟會穿衣服，還會綁鞋帶。My little brother can get dressed, and he can also tie his shoelaces.
…… ，還 ……。
…, and also …
  • 他是我的同學，還是我的好朋友。He is my classmate, and also my good friend.
  • 我喜歡畫畫，還喜歡看書。I like drawing, and I also like reading.
除了 …… 就 ……。
apart from …, (there is) only …
  • 他除了喜歡踢球，就喜歡游泳。Apart from playing football, he only likes swimming.
  • 書包裏除了課本，就只有一枝筆。Apart from textbooks, there is only one pen in the schoolbag.
…… 一 …… ，就 ……。
as soon as …, (then) …
  • 他一到家，就開始做功課。As soon as he gets home, he starts his homework.
  • 弟弟一聽到音樂，就跳起舞來。As soon as my brother hears music, he starts to dance.
…… 得 ……
describes how well an action is done (complement of degree)
  • 我坐得穩。I sit steadily.
  • 他扶得牢。He holds on firmly.
  • 弟弟抓得緊。My brother grips tightly.
